# Taskora App

## Brief Description of the App

### What the app is:
 - Taskora is a productivity and tracking application focused on monitoring tasks, time, and earnings.

### Who it is for:
 - It is designed for students, freelancers, and individuals who want to organize their daily work and track productivity.

### What it helps users achieve:
 - It helps users manage tasks efficiently, track time spent, and understand their productivity and earnings in one place.

## Problem Statement

### What problem does this app address?
 - Many users struggle to track tasks, manage time effectively, and monitor their progress and earnings using separate tools.

### Why is this problem worth solving?
 - Poor task and time management leads to low productivity, missed deadlines, and lack of clarity about effort and results.

## Solution

### How does your app solve this problem?
 - The app provides an integrated platform to manage tasks, track time, and analyze productivity and earnings together.

### What is the core idea behind your solution?
 - Combining task tracking, time tracking, and performance analysis into one simple and organized system.

## Key Features (Initial List)
    - Task creation and management
    - Time tracking for tasks
    - Daily and weekly productivity overview
    - Earnings tracking and summaries
    - Simple and clean user interface

## Value Proposition

### What makes this app useful?
 - It centralizes task management, time tracking, and productivity analysis in one application.

### Why would users choose it?

 - Users choose Taskora because it saves time, improves organization, and provides clear insights into productivity and performance.
